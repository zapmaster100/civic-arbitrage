# GAME FLOW — v0.1

## Setup
1. Load Map 001 (12×7 coordinate system).
2. Place the inland neutral starting town, Plaza Mayor, starting roads and remote neutral beach house.
3. Prepare Population and Jobs tracks at 0 with thresholds 4, 9 and 15.
4. Prepare the private building supply and two market rows of five.
5. Prepare the Municipal staging space and ordered Municipal sequence.
6. Give each player six ownership tokens.
7. Randomize first player.
8. Snake draft two starting parcels free: A-B-C-D-D-C-B-A.
9. Give each player $5 hidden cash.
10. Begin with Player A.

## Turn
Take 2 actions; actions may repeat:
- Acquire parcel.
- Sell parcel.
- Build Road.
- Zone/Rezone.
- Take Building.

Then pass to the next player.

## Acquire
Choose any unowned purchasable parcel. Pay its current land value to the bank and place one ownership token. No adjacency restriction.

## Sell
Choose one of your parcels. Receive its current land value from the bank, remove your token, and make it immediately available to all players. Zoning, building and infrastructure remain.

## Road
Pay $1. Place one road segment connected to the existing shared road network. Road edges provide frontage to adjacent parcels.

## Council
Choose an owned parcel and proposed use/density. Build a 12-cube bag from the four orthogonal neighbours, draw 7, and pass on 4+ Yes. After failure the player may stop or pay $1/$2/$3 for Low/Medium/High and redraw. v0.1 permits repeated paid redraws while affordable.

## Market/build
Choose a row and card. Put $1 on each card skipped. Take the selected card and collect its accumulated money. Either legally build it immediately or discard it.

A legal private build requires sufficient matching-use zoning, frontage, and any density prerequisite. A built private project pays $5 and advances Population or Jobs by 1/2/3. Replace any prior private building and discard the old one.

Collapse the row toward position 1. If a Municipal is staged, it enters position 5; otherwise draw a private replacement.

## Growth and Municipal
Population is Residential growth; Jobs is Commercial growth. Thresholds are 4, 9, 15. Overshoot is retained. A side that crosses ahead freezes further construction of that type until the other side reaches the same threshold.

When both reach a threshold, unlock the next Municipal into staging. Municipal #1 is Low, #2 Medium, #3 High.

Municipals cannot be discarded. Building one requires its Municipal zoning and frontage. It uses normal Council rules. When built, the city buys the parcel at current land value, the owner receives the money and token back, and the civic building remains.

Building Municipal #3 ends the game immediately.

## Endgame
Reveal cash. Add current land value of every parcel still owned. Highest total wealth wins. Tie breaker remains open.
